How reviews are scheduled

Each of your moves is its own card. A move you find on the first try comes back after 1, 3, 7, 16, 35, then 90 days. A miss brings it back in 10 minutes and restarts the count. You reach mastered at the 16-day step. Your progress is saved in this browser; use Back up to keep a copy.

Pieces: cburnett set by Colin M.L. Burnett (CC BY-SA 3.0). Engine: Stockfish 19 Lite (GPLv3) via stockfish.js. Opening names: lichess-org/chess-openings (CC0).
